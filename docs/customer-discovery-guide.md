# Customer discovery and historical-record request

Founder target by November 7: five substantive conversations and two data-sharing commitments. LoadBond-discovery.xlsx supplies 15 developer-treasury, five counsel and five bank/utility target slots. Names, organizations and public contact sources await research. No outreach has been sent.

## Thirty-minute interview

1. Reconstruct the participant's last security posting, increase, reduction and release. Ask for dates and records rather than opinions of the product.
2. Identify who maintains the register, detects expired or superseded terms, resolves conflicting records and approves changes. Document time spent and previous reconciliation costs.
3. Identify when candidate sites were bona fide alternatives for one economic project. Separate committed parallel projects, sequential obligations and delayed releases.
4. Record face, drawable amount, facility usage, restricted cash, guarantees and surety indemnity separately. Ask what the bank actually recorded and whether substitutions offset any proposed benefit.
5. Ask what a diagnostic must prove, who approves its budget and what evidence supports its value. Test fee hypotheses only after documenting customer value.
6. Request a historical-data commitment, authorized reviewer, permitted sharing process and next meeting. Record objections and counterexamples.

Ask counsel which operative instrument terms and governing law support each proposed constraint. Ask banks for written baseline/proposed comparisons of a named capacity or cash metric. Ask utilities for adverse states where protection falls short or payment is delayed. Founder review is not counsel approval.

## Reconstruction workbook

Instrument days accepts one row per project/instrument/calendar day, with actual amount, source version/hash, exact passage and reviewer. Values are dollars, not cents. Retain outstanding instruments until evidenced release. Keep unknown bank and cash metrics blank. Mark alternative status YES only after checking the commercial timeline.

Daily reconstruction sums source rows for the selected project and dates. Missing sources, unresolved currency or duplicate instrument IDs make totals unavailable; a genuine zero remains numeric zero. The template has 200 source rows and 100 output days. Extend table and formula ranges as data grows.

Expand source intervals into instrument-days and split at amendments/reductions. Starts are inclusive and released ends exclusive. Record transformations and reconcile against original registers. Preserve alternative-site overlaps and release delays.

Daily face totals do not establish draw rights or pass a commercial gate. Review distinct sites and consecutive coverage manually. Occurrence requires at least two independent projects each with $25M+ across bona fide alternative sites for 60 consecutive days. After ten adequate projects from three unrelated developers, fewer than two qualifying cases fails the cross-site gate. Incomplete evidence remains inconclusive.
