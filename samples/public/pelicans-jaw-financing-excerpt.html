(v)    For the avoidance of doubt, to the extent issued by the same LC Issuer, the Maximum Interconnection LC Commitment and the Maximum DSR LC Commitment comprise a single set of Commitments. As such Interconnection Letters of Credit (and Unreimbursed Obligations and LC Loans relating thereto), on the one hand, and DSR Letters of Credit (and Unreimbursed Obligations and LC Loans relating thereto), on the other hand, shall not be permitted to be outstanding at the same time. In furtherance of the foregoing, Interconnection Letters of Credit (and Unreimbursed Obligations and LC Loans relating thereto) shall only be permitted to be outstanding prior to the Term Conversion Date and DSR Letters of Credit (and Unreimbursed Obligations and LC Loans relating thereto) shall only be permitted to be outstanding from and after the Term Conversion Date. The failure of any LC Issuer to issue a Letter of Credit requested to be issued by it hereunder shall not relieve any other LC Issuer of its obligations hereunder. Notwithstanding the foregoing or anything to the contrary, (x) Borrower shall request that the Letters of Credit be issued by all LC Issuers with Commitments for the applicable Series on a pro rata basis and (y) the Administrative Agent shall direct the Collateral Agent to, and the Collateral Agent shall, make any drawing on the DSR Letters of Credit on a pro rata basis with respect to the stated amount of all DSR Letters of Credit then in effect.
