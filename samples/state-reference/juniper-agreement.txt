SYNTHETIC REFERENCE CASE. Not a legal agreement or a customer transaction.
Juniper is evaluating a replacement infrastructure connection.
Section 1. On 2026-10-09, Alder Utility holds an issued letter of credit with face USD 9,000,000.
Section 2. A distinct replacement letter of credit with face USD 15,000,000 may be posted to Birch Utility when replacement is authorized.
Section 3. Posting the replacement does not cancel the predecessor; both may remain outstanding until the predecessor release becomes effective.
Section 4. The predecessor is released 30 days after receipt of a valid notice acknowledged by Alder Utility.
Section 5. On 2026-10-12, Alder Utility acknowledged receipt of a valid release notice.
Section 6. If a rating downgrade occurs, each effective instrument's face requirement increases to 125 percent of its otherwise applicable amount.
Section 7. A default does not release security; replacement remains authorized during default.
Section 8. Documentary drawings require the beneficiary's signed demand. The agreement alone does not establish whether the required demand exists.
Section 9. The predecessor automatically extends unless a separately verified termination becomes effective.
Section 10. Completed engineering creates a separate USD 4,000,000 obligation on the predecessor project.
Section 11. Completed replacement engineering creates a distinct USD 3,000,000 obligation.
Section 12. When replacement is authorized, construction creates an additional USD 12,000,000 obligation secured by the same replacement LC as section 11.
Section 13. This agreement establishes modeled security requirements, not bank utilization or restricted cash.
